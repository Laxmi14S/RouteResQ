# 🚀 RouteResQ — Intelligent Journey Recovery System

**When journeys go wrong, find your way forward.**

## 📌 Overview

RouteResQ is a proposed journey-recovery platform designed to help travellers respond to unexpected delays, cancellations, and missed connections.

Instead of searching across multiple platforms for alternatives, travellers can use RouteResQ to evaluate possible recovery plans based on cost, arrival time, transfers, and personal preferences.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

When one part of a multi-leg journey is delayed or cancelled, passengers may miss connecting transport services.

They often need to find alternatives, compare costs, estimate arrival times, and decide how to reach their destination.

RouteResQ aims to simplify this process by identifying connection risks and helping travellers evaluate alternative journeys.

## 💡 Key Features

* **Connection Risk Checker:** Identifies potentially missed connections based on arrival times and transfer requirements.
* **Alternative Journey Finder:** Evaluates possible ways to complete the remaining journey.
* **Budget-Aware Recovery:** Filters alternatives according to the traveller's budget.
* **Multi-Modal Planning:** Designed to compare combinations of supported transport modes.
* **Explainable Recommendations:** Explains the trade-offs between cost, arrival time, and transfers.
* **Journey Management:** Allows users to save and revisit journey plans.

## 🌟 What Makes RouteResQ Different?

Traditional travel planning focuses on finding and booking journeys.

RouteResQ focuses on what happens when an existing journey no longer goes according to plan.

Its proposed approach combines connection-risk analysis, disruption-aware planning, budget constraints, and explainable recommendations in one recovery workflow.

The uniqueness of this approach will be evaluated through competitor research and real-world testing.

## 👥 Target Users

* Travellers with connecting journeys.
* Passengers using multiple transport operators.
* Travel agencies and booking platforms.
* Corporate travel-management providers.

## 💼 Business Potential

RouteResQ could eventually offer journey-recovery capabilities to travel businesses through software subscriptions and API integrations.

Potential benefits include more structured recovery decisions, reduced manual effort, and an improved passenger experience.

These business opportunities will require validation with prospective customers.
